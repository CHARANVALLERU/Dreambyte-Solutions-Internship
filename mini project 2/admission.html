Admission Instructions

Please fill the admission form carefully. This form collects student details, course interest, preferred class timing, and document information.

Correct information helps the academy contact students quickly and guide them to the right course.

Before Filling the Form

  • Check the available courses
  • Keep your email and phone number ready
  • Keep ID proof ready for upload
  • Select your preferred course

Admission Process

  1. Fill basic details
  2. Select courses and learning mode
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Submit the form
  5. Wait for confirmation
